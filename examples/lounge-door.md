# How a door hinge changed a lounge layout

This walkthrough follows a completed concept review for a real lounge. The figures below come from the project’s review record. The homeowner wanted a route through the furnished room while people were seated, a view of the television from each seat and somewhere to put a drink.

## Try the decision

The household chose 700 mm as the target for this modelled route. That figure was specific to this brief. The FreeCAD study kept the furniture and proposed doorway position fixed and changed which jamb carried the hinges. The reviewer reopened the native model before measuring the route.

| Door option | Clearance in the original grid check | Continuous clearance in an independent polygon check |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Hinges on the jamb nearer the window | 740 mm | 746 mm |
| Hinges on the other jamb | 540 mm | 550 mm |

Which option would you carry into the furnished concept? Both checks favour the window-side hinge. On the polygon check it leaves 46 mm above the household target. Reversing the hinge leaves the route 150 mm short. The small difference between methods had no effect on that choice. The door opening and finished clear width still needed measuring on site.

## What else had to work

All four seated positions had a view of the sampled points on the screen. The through-route avoided the central sightline from each position. A separate reach check found that somebody sitting upright on the sofa would need to lean forwards to put down a cup. The homeowner accepted that compromise and kept the existing table. The clear route alone would have missed it.

The reviewer then checked the furnished Blender camera, a reverse view and the finishes in neutral light, reconciling 15 shopping lines with the scene. An early AI image introduced sunlight absent from the approved camera. The reviewer rejected that image. At handover the team compared all 18 files in the issue archive with their sources and reopened the CAD models and packed Blender scene. Site measurements, the proposed opening and physical finish samples still needed professional or homeowner confirmation before building or ordering.
