# Room by Room film brand

Taken from the Ryecroft film's [channel.json](../../../examples/ryecroft-film/film/channel.json) and page on 27 November 2026. Recheck against the repository's README and [logo](../../../docs/brand/room-by-room.svg) before a new film.

## Palette

The film is drawn on warm paper with one deep green.

| Role | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Ground | `#f3f1eb` |
| Deep ground | `#e2ded2` |
| Ink | `#1f3833` |
| Accent, line and dot | `#205d48` |
| Second accent | `#386754` |
| Card and card ink | `#ffffff`, `#1f3833` |
| Chat panel | `#e6ebe7` |
| Trial flag | `#d9a441` |
| AI concept flag | `#c9d4ce` |

The run's own drawings use the same family: green for the chosen option, amber for the alternative and rust for a warning. Keep a real output's own colours. Do not recolour a result to match the film.

## Type

| Use | Face |
| --- | --- |
| Headlines, card titles, the wordmark | Georgia, weight 800, letter spacing -0.02em |
| Body and chat | Segoe UI, then Arial |
| Calls, tags, flags and figures | Consolas, then Courier New |

These are system fonts on the rendering machine and are not shipped. A render elsewhere substitutes its own, so render the delivered file on a machine that has them or choose open faces and ship them with their licence.

## Wordmark and cards

- **Wordmark.** Live text `room-by-room` in Georgia 800 beside the accent dot. The film does not use a logo image. The floor plan logo in `docs/brand/` came later and may open or close a new film.
- **End card.** The wordmark, `github.com/1749-io/room-by-room` and the line "Open source. Concepts to brief your professional team."
- **Credit line.** "Ryecroft is an example house, not a real one · Music and sound synthesised for this film · FreeCAD, Blender, QGIS, EnergyPlus and Codex belong to their owners". Rewrite it for each film from what that film used.
- **Closing card.** "Agents by 1749.io", "Custom agents and workflows, built for your company", "1749.io". It holds for 4.5 seconds after the end card.
- **Thumbnail.** 1280 × 720: the title on a paper band, then one camera shown three ways as FreeCAD, Blender and the AI concept photo, each labelled.

## Format

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Style | make-film channel-thread, with no voiceover |
| Frame | 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 30 frames a second |
| Tempo | 95 beats a minute. Moves between cards take 0.48 and 0.62 seconds |
| Length | About 2 minutes 40 seconds |
| Sound | Synthesised music and cues, mastered to -14 LUFS with a true peak at or below -1 dBTP |
| Signature | One accent line drawn through every card, ending under the pack |

## Voice

- Plain British English in chat lines and captions. People talk like a household, not like a brochure.
- Card titles name the tool or the decision: "FreeCAD", "A choice", "The pack".
- A call strip gives the tool and, in a few words, what it did: "FreeCAD MCP builds the model".
- Figures carry their unit and their limit: "756 mm at the kitchen door", "trial case for a structural engineer".
- No superlatives and no promises about a finished home. The film shows concepts to brief a professional team.
