# Check the light and the selected finishes

Run this review for a furnished room before releasing its final AI camera. The homeowner’s chosen time of day, product variants and source photographs determine the comparison.

1. Save the baseline camera, lens, exposure, view transform and white balance. Identify daylight openings and any lamps actually used. In a copy, test the proposed lighting from that camera with the same furnishings and surface materials. Compare direction, fall-off, shadows, clipped highlights and colour cast with the room photos. A daylight scene can use a measured outdoor HDRI from Poly Haven Lite or another suitable source. Orient it to the room where the evidence allows. Check that exterior scenery admits light and record assumptions about orientation or weather.
2. Review a neutral-light view of each prominent selected item beside its exact supplier variant or a photographed physical swatch. Check silhouette, construction, colour, scale and direction of the weave or grain, roughness and sheen. Preserve distinctive details such as seams and feet. Give a generic scanned surface the correct physical scale and mapping. For a named product, use a library map only for the properties it matches. Record the asset, licence, mapping and affected objects. Mark unconfirmed finishes as approximations.
3. Open the furnished camera and reverse view to review the colours together. Compare adjacent finishes, accents and the visible adjoining space with the requested mood and focal points. Record the view, light and finding in a current `room-palette` row. Identify the smallest adjustment for any concern and raise an open homeowner choice before final AI.
4. Adjust the first stage that fails and repeat the affected comparisons. Reopen the saved scene with its textures available. Attach the original camera, lighting-only comparison, neutral finish review and final camera evidence to the room record. A layout or product change returns to its earlier gate. The final AI image inherits the passed camera, with an independent check of the actual image.

Poly Haven is optional. A windowless room, evening brief or a workstation without a library connection may use appropriate practical lights, sourced textures or a documented procedural match. Apply the same tests. [Poly Haven lighting guide](https://docs.polyhaven.com/en/guides/hdris) · [Texture scale and maps](https://docs.polyhaven.com/en/technical-standards/textures)

For a colour discrepancy, compare the product reference, neutral product view, lit Blender camera and final AI image in that order. Record where the discrepancy first appears. If every finish shifts together, inspect the light and colour management before adjusting individual materials. The [colour review and decorator guidance](../../renovation-pack/references/colour-decisions.md) gives the required evidence for the finished image and the separate physical paint decision.
