# Living with a highly glazed room

A room review can be triggered by “what is next?” or “design this room”. Look at the plan, roof and photographs when the room is named. For a conservatory, sunroom, orangery, winter garden or another room where substantial glazing drives use, start this study alongside existing CAD. In another room, such as a kitchen under a glazed roof, put the standalone line `Glazing review: required` in its room README. The named room types are recognised automatically. Screen again after changes to openings, trees, shading, occupancy or use.

Start with the actual household activity, the seated or standing viewpoint and when it matters. Read these from the brief. When breakfast, afternoon reading and year-round work would lead to materially different designs, show the trade-off and ask which activity matters. Compare at least one usable layout and its affordable adaptations. Return furniture variant, dimensions, cost and care to the room schedule. Keep doors, thresholds, ventilation openings, cleaning, safe access and shade controls operable with someone in place.

| Current layout finding | What the reviewer records |
| --- | --- |
| `external-shadow` | Match the actual plot to photographs and an aligned map. Record true north, confidence, roof planes and the location of the seat. Check buildings, terrain, hedges and tree crowns. Where data permits, use a dated surface and terrain raster in a common CRS and height datum. Trace rays from the occupied point. Compare present obstructions with a plausible canopy sensitivity, keeping buildings in place. Report local clock times, seasonal foliage, map age and the deciding site check. |
| `seated-glare` | Check the sun relative to the eyes, gaze and task, including low sun through end panes and high sun through the roof. Separate direct-sun potential from a measured glare rating. Test turning the chair, a local adjustable blind or another working position. Check the garden view and operation of openings. |
| `summer-heat` | Compare baseline, external shade, ventilation and their combination under the same local weather and occupied hours where a room model is justified. Document glazing, roof, air changes, obstructions and deployment. Distinguish an assumed control from one the household can actually use during rain, absence or overnight. |
| `winter-cold` | Establish heated or seasonal use, separation from the house, night-time losses, draughts and condensation. Test winter conditions and the effect on plants, finishes and the adjoining room. Return a comfort intervention or an explicitly seasonal use. |

Reference the actual evidence in the same saved revision in `layout-checks.csv`. Give each finding a source, expectation, observed consequence and result. The normal proposed-CAD gate also covers furnished release. Unless the evidence supports the intended use, record a hold, the evidence owner and what will be rechecked. A preliminary design can still show conditional options. Use the tools appropriate to the available evidence, including a section or bounded comparison when a weather model would give spurious precision. Keep actual temperature, formal glare metrics, compliance and permission for works separate from concept findings.
