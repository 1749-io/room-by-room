# Handover checks for a renovation concept

Before final AI, record how the palette of each furnished room works against its brief. Consult [the colour decision rules](colour-decisions.md) for the evidence, homeowner choices, final-image colour fidelity and physical finish issue. Record actual discrepancies in the affected visual gate and pending physical samples on the decorator schedule.

In a house workbook, run `python house.py check-render "House" "Room" blender --input-revision R3` before starting the furnished camera and use `final-ai --input-revision R3` before generating the finish study. Substitute the revision read from the actual input. Set the affected stage to hold when the homeowner pauses visuals. Record its release before resuming.

Use the actual input revision on workbook checks. An optional unrendered furniture study follows an accepted existing shell and a `check-study` pass. Return candidate poses to proposed CAD and repeat its occupied-use checks. Record the live connector, native file, revision, measured features and discrepancies for scripted builds and placements.

Maintain a review record per room and revision. For each handover, record the input file or product source, the check actually performed, pass or hold, the evidence a reviewer can inspect, and any correction required. A changed wall, product variant, material or camera reopens the affected downstream checks. If a shared route changes, reopen the adjoining rooms, their purchase or fit decisions and any affected audience sheets. Before furnishing, run the workbook's check-layout command for the room and respect any separate render hold. A room on hold can appear in a diagnostic review labelled as such. Only passed views enter the finished presentation.

| Handover | Evidence required for a pass | Correction route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client brief to room scope | Walk the original plan, email, attachments and later instructions. Check the house limits and room exceptions. A consequential option needs authority to explore it and a recorded choice before release as the selected design. Tie each room, shared activity zone, retained item and selected option to a decision. Reconcile all shopping rows, including shared items, reuse, alternatives and omissions. | Correct the scope or resolve the specific conflicting decision. |
| Existing-condition CAD | Orient the plan from identifiable openings and adjacent rooms. Compare known dimensions, levels and wall faces. Walk the perimeter against available photographs in both directions. Identify projections, recesses, hearths, radiators, services, window sills, door leaves and overhead restrictions that affect the brief. Save a plan and a section or 3D cutaway keyed to the sources. Record each consequential discrepancy, uncertain dimension and affected decision. | Model observed features provisionally where their dimensions are unknown. Test a plausible range if supported by evidence. Hold a fit decision when the missing measurement could reverse it. |
| Proposed-layout CAD | Review an existing/proposed overlay showing retained, removed and new work. Compare the plan with the room-specific relationships and use cases in the [layout review](layout-review.md). Walk through the client's actual uses with dimensioned furniture: enter with the door open, sit and rise, pull a chair out, open storage, use appliances and reach adjoining rooms. Dimension the tightest point on each critical route, including the doorway. Use sections for height-sensitive decisions. Check access to retained fixtures and services. Save the reviewed plan, cutaway, limiting dimensions and findings for this revision. | Correct conflicts in CAD and repeat affected scenarios. Identify structural or survey dependencies explicitly. Release affected Blender cameras only after the existing geometry and proposed use both pass for the intended concept decision. |
| CAD to Blender | Check a known length and axis, transferred room boundaries, doors, windows, floor and ceiling. Inspect the exact camera at full size for clipping and views through missing fabric. Compare camera, plan and CAD together. | Correct the geometry or camera, re-export and repeat the comparison. |
| Shopping schedule to furnished Blender | Record the item ID, exact name and variant, quantity, manufacturer or retailer reference, dimensions, material and visible distinguishing features. Link the named scene objects and their evidence. Use the actual manufacturer model where available, or reconstruct against product dimensions and photographs. Confirm quantity across the room, including reused pieces and items borrowed between rooms. Mark intentionally out-of-frame items and unselected options. | A sized block can pass a space-planning check. It stays a proxy until a side-by-side product comparison supports its visible identity. If evidence is unavailable, keep the affected finish view on hold or show a clearly labelled layout study. |
| Blender to final AI image | Check each selected or reused item against the furnished scene and record what is visible in each camera. Use a reverse angle when an important area falls outside the main view. Save the full-resolution Blender render as the primary input, with labelled product references for identity and any original concept image labelled with the intended styling and spatial relationships it supplies. Generate the final lighting and material treatment directly from that render. | For a layout change, correct CAD; for missing furnishings, dress Blender and render the camera again. For an AI finish error, retry from the saved Blender camera and references. Keep any intermediate AI pilot as a separate comparison. |
| Final AI image to homeowner pack | A full concept pack includes a final AI finish image for each agreed hero view. Review at presentation size beside the original Blender camera and the product references. Compare room boundaries, door/window positions, glazing divisions, fixture counts, chair orientation, furniture proportions, feet, upholstery, finishes and clear routes. Check for merged objects, impossible reflections, phantom openings and illegible details. Record the result per camera and prominent item. | Reject a material discrepancy. Correct the responsible stage or composite the verified Blender product back into the image and recheck it. A shopping link or AI prompt alone cannot establish product fidelity. Keep rejected attempts outside issued deliverables. |
| Audience editions to release | Trace every published finish image to a passed camera review and its shopping references. Reopen both native models, test-render a named camera, inspect the exported presentation and extract the actual handover archive. Check the purchase schedule retains current decision status. | Keep development files available as drafts. Publish the final room or whole-pack issue only with the applicable checks recorded. |

Before releasing the furnished camera, use the Blender skill’s lighting and finish review. Save an unchanged-camera lighting comparison, a neutral-light comparison for prominent shopping variants and asset provenance. Compare the daylight or practical lights with the room evidence and inspect the final camera after any material change. The library and the lighting design may vary by house.

At final furnishing review reconcile the complete scheduled room count to named native objects, then mark visibility separately for each camera. A reverse view can evidence pieces outside the hero frame. Keep optional, omitted and incidental items identifiable. Compare the AI output with the furnished image for both missing and newly introduced pieces. If the homeowner adopts an AI suggestion, source and model it and repeat the relevant fit and camera checks. A final photo passes once the recorded comparison passes.

For uncertain product identity, show the supplier reference beside the rendered object and state exactly which features are unresolved. Colour under simulated daylight and retailer photography is approximate. Physical swatches remain the reference for purchase decisions.

At drawing review size, check that each label clears walls, door arcs, dimension lines and adjacent text. Move crowded notes into the margin and inspect the exported sheet again. Before accepting a room layout, compare both camera directions with available listing photographs and record fixed features omitted from the floor plan, especially chimney breasts, hearths and radiators.

At handover, open the exported issue as its intended recipient. Use the [pack content guide](deliverables.md) to check that the reader can locate the decision, follow it to the evidence and identify the next action. Cross-check repeated revisions, dimensions, product variants, quantities, costs and holds against their source records. Record the exported filename, revision and what you verified in the existing handover finding or its linked evidence.
